Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026. Last updated: November 12, 2026.

Short version: Enough does not collect your data. Everything the app stores stays on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to me or to anyone else.

1. Who is responsible

Enough is developed and published by Mateo Parra Calderón, an individual developer based in Colombia. You can reach me at mateo.parra.calderon@icloud.com.

2. What data the app handles, and where

Enough stores the following only on your device, in a storage area shared between the app and its own extensions (the usage monitor, the block screen and the widget):

  • Your settings: use and break lengths for the cycles, daily limit, alert times, daily reset time, emergency-use length, and whether the lock is on (and, if you asked to turn it off, when it will turn off).
  • Your selection of apps, categories and websites, saved as opaque tokens created by Apple's picker. They are not readable as app names.
  • Status: minutes used in the current cycle and today in your limited apps, each as a single total; whether your apps are available, on a break, blocked or in emergency use; and when the current break or block ends.
  • An event log with diagnostic entries (for example, that a break started or that a block ended) and their timestamps.
  • A clock reference (the time, how long the device has been running and an identifier of the current start-up) used to notice when the device clock is moved forward.

This information never leaves your device on its own. There is no server and no account, and the app makes no network connections of its own. The one exception is buying or checking an Enough Pro subscription, which goes through Apple (see section 7).

3. What Enough does not do

  • It does not collect personal data, and it does not transmit any data off your device.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it has no data to sell or share.
  • It does not track you, within or across apps and websites.
  • It has no accounts or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.

4. How Enough uses Apple's Screen Time technologies

Enough uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Device Activity and Managed Settings) in individual mode, to set a limit on your own device. Apple's picker returns your choices to the app as opaque identifiers, so Enough does not know which apps, categories or websites you chose, and it cannot see your usage history. Apple processes that usage information on your device, and the system tells Enough only that a time threshold you configured has been reached. Enough asks for your authorization the first time you set it up. See Apple's own documentation and privacy policy for how Apple handles Screen Time data.

5. Notifications

Enough can show notifications such as "N minutes left". They are scheduled locally on your device. They do not use a push server, and they are optional: you can decline them or turn them off in iOS Settings.

6. Event log

The event log is stored on your device to help diagnose problems. It records things like changes of state, the limits you saved, how many apps, categories and websites are in your selection, and whether a notification was delivered. Entries do not contain the names of your apps, and old entries are discarded automatically as the log grows. It leaves your phone only if you decide to share it yourself, for example with the share button in the app's Event log screen when you write to support.

7. Purchases (Enough Pro)

Enough is free. The lock is part of Enough Pro, an optional auto-renewing monthly subscription that is bought, billed, renewed and cancelled entirely by Apple through the App Store, using your Apple Account. I do not receive or store your payment information, your name, your Apple Account or any other identity data.

The app only asks Apple, through Apple's StoreKit framework, whether you have an active subscription, so it knows whether to allow the lock. The app itself keeps no purchase history and sends nothing to me or to any server of mine. If the subscription ends, the lock turns itself off and any block from the lock mode is released; the free mode stays available. Apple's own terms and privacy policy apply to the purchase, and you can manage or cancel the subscription in iOS Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

8. Retention and deletion

The data described above stays on your device until you change your settings or delete the app. Deleting Enough removes all of it.

So that a block cannot be skipped, Enough changes two iOS settings through Screen Time. While a break, a block or an emergency use is in progress, apps cannot be deleted from the iPhone (this applies to every app, including Enough); that ends as soon as the break or block ends, and then you can delete Enough. And while you have a limit set up, Enough asks iOS to keep the date and time set automatically. Both stop when you remove Enough's Screen Time permission or delete the app.

To revoke the Screen Time permission, open iOS Settings, then Screen Time, then Apps With Screen Time Access, and remove Enough. Without that permission, Enough can no longer limit anything.

9. Children

Enough does not collect data from anyone, including children and minors.
