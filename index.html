Enough

A daily time limit for the apps that take too much of your day. It runs entirely on your iPhone.

What it does

  • You choose a group of apps, categories or websites with Apple's own picker, and they share one daily limit.
  • You get a heads-up before time runs out, and a block screen when it does.
  • One emergency use per block, measured in minutes of use.
  • An optional strict mode that freezes your settings so a limit is harder to undo on impulse.
  • A widget that shows minutes used and minutes left.

Private by design

No accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your settings and usage stay on your device. Enough never learns which apps you picked.
